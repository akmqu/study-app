# Student Payments & Billing

## Overview (`/student/payments`)
* **Active Plan Summary:** Shows current monthly fee, included sessions, and next billing date.
* **Payment Action:** A "Pay Now" button to settle outstanding balances via integrated payment gateways.
* **Payment Method:** Displays linked card details with an option to update them.
* **Payment History Table:** A detailed log of past payments containing period, date, payment method, amount, and status (Paid / Pending).
